# Executive Committee photos

Drop a square portrait here named after the committee member's id in
`src/data/committee.ts` and it appears automatically on the homepage
leadership section and the Executive Committee page:

    bikash-kadayat.jpg
    sadikshya-rijal.jpg
    sambridhi-subedi.jpg
    nirmal-bk.jpg
    sanjita-shrestha.jpg
    jenisha-basnet.jpg
    suman-karki.jpg

Accepted formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp (checked in that order).
Recommended: 600 x 600 px, face centred, under 300 KB.
Members without a photo get an initials avatar, so nothing breaks while
photos are still being collected.
